Topshark Shorts Uploader

A private upload tool for the Seika Sunswirl YouTube channel.

What it is

Topshark Shorts Uploader is a small private tool that the owner of the Seika Sunswirl (サンスワール せいか) Ch. YouTube channel uses to upload and schedule their own short videos on their own channel. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Sets each video's title, description, privacy setting and publish time.
  • Reads the status of the channel's own uploads to confirm they went through.

It requests only these Google permissions: youtube.upload (upload videos) and youtube.readonly (read the channel's own video status).

Privacy

The tool stores only an OAuth token on the owner's own computer. No data is shared or sold. Read the full Privacy Policy.
